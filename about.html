About me

Building, fixing, and learning how things work.

I chose engineering because I like building and reassembling things, solving problems, and seeing a clear result when something works again.

01 / My story

I like finding and fixing the problem.

I’ve assembled and repaired TVs, TV boxes, phones, PCs and laptops. I’ve also worked on car engines, motors, and other electronics. I’m drawn to understanding what is wrong, working through the problem, and getting to the moment when it works again.

I’m now studying Electrical and Electronics Engineering at ADA University and building on that practical experience through my coursework.
